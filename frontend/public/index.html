How it works

05 features
01 / 05
Instant Registration
Receptionist enters patient name → token issued atomically → QR generated → patient added to live queue. All in under a second.
02 / 05
QR Personal Tracker
Patients scan their QR and see their live position, how many are ahead, and estimated wait time — all updating in real time.
03 / 05
Live Wait Estimation
Dynamically calculated: Patients Ahead × Avg. Consultation Time. Configurable. Updates on every queue event automatically.
04 / 05
Skip & Recall
Patient unresponsive? Skip them. Need to re-announce? Recall the token. Full queue control at the receptionist's fingertips.
05 / 05
Multi-Clinic Isolation
Each clinic operates in its own Socket.IO room. Scale to any number of branches without interference. Production-ready by design.
